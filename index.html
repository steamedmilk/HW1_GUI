Old School RuneScape

What is Old School RuneScape?

OSRS is an MMORPG, it is a game that allows users from all over the world to be able to connect and play together real time. It was released in 2013 developed and published by Jagex. It began as a rerelease of the game based on its 2007 version prior to its sequel RuneScape 3.

Features

  • Open World: Allows players to explore the world of Gielinor unrestricted
  • Skills: 23 different skills to train and experience
  • Quests: 170 unique quests
  • PVE: Large variety of content battling against different monsters and bosses

For more information you can visit Old School RuneScape
